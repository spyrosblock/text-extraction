Refund policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Credit packs are sold through Stripe's Managed Payments: Stripe, through Link, is the merchant of record. Refunds are paid back through Stripe to the card or account you paid with, and Link sends you a refund note.

Unused credits: 14 days

Within 14 days of a purchase you can ask for a refund of the credits from it that you haven't used. We refund the share of the pack that is still on the key: for example, if you bought 2,000 credits for €10 and the key still has 1,500 of them, you get €7.50 back. After 14 days purchases aren't refunded, except where the law requires it; your credits don't expire, so you can keep using them.

If you are a consumer in the European Union you have a 14-day right to withdraw from the purchase. This policy refunds the unused part of a purchase in those same 14 days, whether or not you are a consumer.

Over the account's credit limit: automatic

An account can hold a limited number of credits, summed over its keys. If a payment would take your account over it, for example because you paid on two payment pages opened at the same time, that payment is refunded in full automatically and its credits aren't added. You don't need to ask; the refund reaches your payment method in the usual time for it.

Not refunded

  • Credits you have used.
  • Credits spent on PDFs that failed or whose pages couldn't be read.
  • Free credits.
  • Credits on a key you revoked, or in an account you deleted: they are forfeited.

What a refund does to your credits

When a payment is refunded, in full or in part, the same share of its credits is taken off the key it was bought for. The same happens when the bank of the card holder reverses a payment (a chargeback) and the dispute is lost: all the credits from that purchase are taken back. If they were already spent, the key's balance goes negative and the key stops working until it is topped up.

How to ask

Write to from the email address of your account, with the receipt number from Link's receipt (or the date and amount of the payment) and the last four characters of the key. For questions about the payment itself you can also contact Link's support from your receipt.
