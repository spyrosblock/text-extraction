Privacy

This is what the service keeps about you, for how long, and how to delete it. Everything is stored in Microsoft Azure in Europe.

What we store

  • Your email address, with your account, so you can sign in. It is used only to send you sign-in links, through Azure Communication Services.
  • Your API keys, as a one-way hash, never the key itself, with each key's name, its last four characters, its credit balance and the credit packs added to it.
  • Sign-in links and sessions, as hashes: links expire after 15 minutes, sessions after 7 days.
  • Your PDFs and their text. A PDF that needs OCR is kept only until it has been read. The extracted text, and the record of which key submitted it, are kept for a day, then deleted.
  • Logs, kept for 30 days: requests with their key and account ids, pages and credits charged, and IP addresses. Email addresses appear in logs only as a hash.
  • Rate-limit counters per IP address and per (hashed) email address, kept for a day.

Your browser keeps your session in its local storage until you sign out. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

Deleting your data

Sign in and choose Delete account on the dashboard. It revokes your keys (their remaining credits are lost) and deletes your account and email address at once. Text and logs expire as above.

One thing is kept: a keyed hash of your email address, recording that it has had its free credits, so that deleting an account and signing up again doesn't grant them again. The hash doesn't contain your address and can't be reversed; it can only be matched by someone who already knows your address and has the service's secret key.
