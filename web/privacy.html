Privacy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This is what the service keeps about you, for how long, and how to delete it. The service is run by the operator named in the terms. Everything we store is in Microsoft Azure in Europe.

What we store

  • Your email address, with your account, so you can sign in. It is used only to send you sign-in links, through Azure Communication Services.
  • Your API keys, as a one-way hash, never the key itself, with each key's name, its last four characters and its credit balance.
  • Your purchases, with the key they were bought for: Stripe's payment id, the pack, its credits, the date, and the credits taken back after a refund or a chargeback. They are deleted with the key.
  • Sign-in links and sessions, as hashes: links expire after 15 minutes, sessions after 7 days.
  • Your PDFs and their text. A PDF that needs OCR is kept only until it has been read. The extracted text, and the record of which key submitted it, are kept for a day, then deleted.
  • Logs, kept for 30 days: requests with their key and account ids, pages and credits charged, payments and refunds with their Stripe ids, and IP addresses. Email addresses appear in logs only as a hash, except when a payment can't be added to its key (for example, a key revoked during the payment): then the payer's address is logged so we can sort it out.
  • Rate-limit counters per IP address and per (hashed) email address, kept for a day.

Your browser keeps your session in its local storage until you sign out. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

Payments

Credit packs are sold through Stripe's Managed Payments: Stripe, through Link, is the merchant of record. You pay on Stripe's page, not on this site, and we never see your card details. We send Stripe your account's email address, to fill in the payment form. Stripe and Link process your name, email and billing address, payment details and tax information as controllers, under their own policies: the Stripe privacy policy and the Link privacy policy. Their payment page sets its own cookies. From Stripe we receive the payment's id, the pack bought, its amount and status, and any refund or dispute.

Deleting your data

Sign in and choose Delete account on the dashboard. It revokes your keys (their remaining credits are lost) and deletes your account and email address at once. Text and logs expire as above.

One thing is kept: a keyed hash of your email address, recording that it has had its free credits, so that deleting an account and signing up again doesn't grant them again. The hash doesn't contain your address and can't be reversed; it can only be matched by someone who already knows your address and has the service's secret key.

Stripe and Link keep payment records for as long as the law requires them to, also after you delete your account here.
