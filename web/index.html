Text out of your PDFs, over HTTP

Send a PDF, get the text of every page back as JSON. Pages with a text layer are read directly; scanned pages are OCRed (English and Greek). PDFs up to 90 MB and 200 pages.

Sign in with your email to create an API key. Your first key comes with 100 free credits.

Prices

1 creditper page with a text layer
5 creditsper page that needs OCR

A PDF is charged when you submit it. Credits are not refunded if a page can't be read.

Quick start

1. Submit a PDF with your key. The response has a request_id, the credits charged and your balance.


    
2. Fetch the text with the same key. Pages that need OCR take a little while; until they're
      done this answers 404, so retry.

    

    
Each page comes back as {"page", "text", "confidence"}:
      "-" for text from the text layer, Tesseract's confidence (0–100) for OCRed
      pages. Text is kept for a day.
